Customer personal data is collected only for order processing, support, and service improvement purposes.
We do not sell customer data to third parties under any circumstances.
Customers can request deletion of their personal data by contacting privacy@kohler.com at any time.
Data is retained for 24 months after the last transaction, then automatically and permanently deleted.
Personal data includes name, contact information, purchase history, and support interaction records.
Payment information is processed through secure, PCI-compliant third-party payment processors and is not stored directly.
Customers have the right to request a copy of all personal data held about them, provided free of charge.
Marketing communications are sent only to customers who have explicitly opted in and can be unsubscribed at any time.
Data may be shared with logistics partners solely for the purpose of order fulfillment and delivery.
All employee access to customer data is logged and subject to periodic internal audit.
In the event of a data breach, affected customers will be notified within 72 hours as required by regulation.
Cookies and website tracking data are used only for improving user experience and are governed by a separate cookie policy.
Customers under the age of 16 must have parental consent before any personal data is collected.
Data collected through customer support calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes, with prior notice.
Anonymized and aggregated data may be used for internal analytics and is not considered personal data under this policy.
Customers can withdraw consent for optional data processing activities without affecting their ability to use core services.
Personal data is stored on secure servers with encryption both at rest and in transit.
Third-party vendors handling customer data on our behalf are contractually bound to equivalent privacy standards.
This privacy policy is reviewed annually and updated to reflect changes in law or business practice.
Any changes to this policy will be communicated to customers via email and posted on the company website.
