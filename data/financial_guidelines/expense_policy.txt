Employees can claim reimbursement for business travel expenses up to ₹40,000 per trip without prior approval.
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred, with itemized receipts attached.
Client entertainment expenses require manager approval before booking, regardless of amount.
Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days via direct deposit to the employee's registered account.
Airfare bookings must be made through the approved corporate travel portal to qualify for reimbursement.
Mileage for personal vehicle use on company business is reimbursed at the current standard company rate per kilometer.
Expenses submitted without valid receipts may be reimbursed at management discretion up to a ₹2,000 cap per item.
International travel expenses must be converted to Indian Rupees using the exchange rate on the date of purchase.
Alcohol expenses are reimbursable only as part of approved client entertainment, up to a reasonable per-person limit.
Home office equipment purchases require pre-approval and are capped at ₹25,000 per employee per year.
Corporate credit card statements must be reconciled monthly by the cardholder through the finance portal.
Expenses related to personal development, such as approved courses or certifications, are reimbursed at 100% upon completion.
Any expense exceeding ₹80,000 requires a second-level approval from a department director.
Duplicate or unclear expense submissions will be returned to the employee for clarification before processing.
Per diem rates for domestic travel are set annually and published on the finance intranet page.
Employees found to have submitted fraudulent expense claims are subject to disciplinary action up to termination.
Petty cash reimbursements are capped at ₹4,000 and must be logged with the office administrator.
Software or SaaS subscriptions for work purposes must be approved by the relevant department head before purchase.
All expense reports must be submitted through the official finance system, not via email or informal channels.
Year-end expense submissions must be finalized by December 15th to be processed within the current fiscal year.
