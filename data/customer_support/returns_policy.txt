Products can be returned within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt or proof of purchase.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method used at checkout.
Items must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging to qualify for a standard return.
For defective products, customers should contact support for a free replacement instead of a standard return.
Customized or made-to-order products are non-returnable unless found to be defective on arrival.
Return shipping costs are covered by the company only in cases of defective or incorrect items shipped.
Customers must initiate a return request through the online portal before shipping any item back.
A return authorization number is required and must be included with the returned package.
Exchanges are permitted within the same 30-day window, subject to product availability.
Installation services, once performed, are non-refundable, though the product itself remains eligible for return.
Bulk or trade orders above a certain quantity threshold are subject to a separate commercial returns policy.
Gift returns without a receipt will be issued as store credit at the item's current listed price.
Products purchased during clearance or final-sale promotions are not eligible for return or exchange.
Warranty claims are handled separately from returns and may extend well beyond the 30-day return window.
Customers can track the status of their return or refund through their online account dashboard.
Damaged-in-transit items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence.
International returns may be subject to additional customs and shipping considerations.
Support representatives are authorized to make case-by-case exceptions for long-standing customers.
All returned items are inspected upon receipt before a refund or exchange is finalized.
Store credit issued from returns does not expire and can be used on any future purchase.
