All vendor contracts must be reviewed by the legal team before signing, regardless of contract value.
Kohler complies with GDPR and CCPA data protection regulations across all regions of operation.
Employees must complete annual compliance training on anti-bribery and anti-corruption policies.
Any suspected compliance violation must be reported to the Ethics Hotline within 48 hours of discovery.
Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the compliance team before engaging in any related business activity.
All marketing claims must be reviewed and approved by legal counsel prior to public release.
Employees are prohibited from accepting gifts from vendors or clients exceeding a nominal value threshold.
Whistleblower reports made in good faith are protected from retaliation under company policy.
Data retention schedules must comply with both internal policy and applicable regional legal requirements.
All intellectual property created during employment belongs to the company unless otherwise contractually specified.
Export control regulations must be reviewed before shipping products to restricted or sanctioned countries.
Any litigation or legal claim against the company must be immediately escalated to the legal department.
Third-party audits of compliance practices are conducted annually by an independent external firm.
Employees handling regulated products must maintain relevant certifications and renew them as required.
All advertising and promotional content must comply with regional consumer protection laws.
Non-disclosure agreements are mandatory for any employee or contractor accessing confidential business information.
Environmental compliance standards apply to all manufacturing facilities and are audited on a quarterly basis.
Any government inquiry or subpoena received must be routed directly to legal before any response is issued.
Compliance policy updates are communicated to all employees within 30 days of regulatory changes.
Failure to comply with documented legal or ethical standards may result in disciplinary action, up to termination.
