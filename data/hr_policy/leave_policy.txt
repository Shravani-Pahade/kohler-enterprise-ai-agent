Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.5 days.
Sick leave requires a medical certificate for absences longer than 2 consecutive working days.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance via the HR portal, except in emergencies.
Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.
Any leave beyond the carry-forward limit will be forfeited unless approved by a department head.
New employees become eligible for paid leave after completing 90 days of continuous employment.
Maternity leave is granted for 26 weeks with full pay, in accordance with company policy and applicable law.
Paternity leave is granted for 2 weeks with full pay, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.
Bereavement leave of up to 5 days is granted for the loss of an immediate family member.
Employees working remotely are subject to the same leave policies as those working on-site.
Public holidays observed by the company are listed annually on the HR portal and do not count against annual leave.
Half-day leave requests are permitted and are deducted as 0.5 days from the annual leave balance.
Employees on probation are eligible for leave but require manager approval for any leave exceeding 2 days.
Leave taken without prior approval, except in documented emergencies, may result in a formal warning.
Employees returning from extended leave (over 30 days) must complete a re-onboarding check-in with HR.
Compensatory leave may be granted for approved overtime work during company-recognized busy periods.
All leave balances reset at the start of the calendar year, except for the permitted carry-forward days.
Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rated basis according to their contracted working hours.
Leave encashment is available only at the time of final settlement upon resignation or termination.
Managers are responsible for ensuring adequate team coverage before approving overlapping leave requests.
